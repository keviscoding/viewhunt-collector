Definitions

Stripe trials = subscriptions with status trialing (card on file).

Product trials = app free challenge (7 days or 3 ranking videos) from Mongo — not Stripe.

Gross / net sales = sum of Stripe paid invoices in range (net subtracts refunds).

Conversions = Stripe subscriptions whose trial_end fell in range and are no longer trialing.

Unique visitors = sum of Cloudflare daily uniques (not period-deduped).
